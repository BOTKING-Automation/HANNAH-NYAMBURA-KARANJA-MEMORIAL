Her Final
Farewell

For everyone who wishes they had a little more time with Hannah, this page holds the farewell details and a place to remember a life that meant so much.

We gather in honour of the Late Hannah Nyambura Karanja, lovingly known as Wa Ruth, with hearts full of love and heavy with her absence. A farewell can bring people together, but no programme can measure the place she held in our lives. This page shares the practical details once they are confirmed, while making room for the grief and gratitude every person carries.

WHEN WE SAY GOODBYE

A farewell is a moment.
Love is a lifetime.

We may gather for one day to say goodbye, but we will remember her in countless days afterwards—in family stories, in prayers said quietly, in familiar songs, and in moments when we wish we could share the news with her first.

May those who gather find comfort in one another. May no one feel pressure to be strong every minute. And may the love we share in honour of Wa Ruth continue long after the final prayer is spoken.

“A life held in love is never remembered only once.”

Tributes & Remembrance

A gentle place to say what still lives in your heart: the thank-you you never finished, the memory you keep replaying, the prayer you whisper, and the love that has nowhere to go but into words.

A tribute does not have to be perfect or polished. It can be one sentence through tears, a memory that still makes you laugh, a lesson you carry quietly, or the words you wish you could tell Hannah today. For those who loved her, Wa Ruth remains in the heart—in stories that refuse to fade, in prayers she helped inspire, and in the ache of wishing for one more conversation.

How this works: You can write a tribute below, copy it, download it, or share it through WhatsApp. This GitHub Pages site does not have a shared database, so submissions are not automatically sent to the family or published for other visitors. Please share your tribute with the family for review before publication.

WORDS FROM THE HEART
Write the words
love still wants to say.
Sometimes the most honest tribute begins with, “I miss you.” Sometimes it begins with “Thank you.” You do not have to make your grief beautiful or find the perfect words. Let the message be true to your heart.
If you are writing to Wa Ruth, take your time. Tell her what her love meant. Name the small thing you miss. Say what you wish she could see. A life remembered with honesty is a life honoured with love.
“Some goodbyes leave a thousand words behind.”

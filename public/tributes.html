Tributes & Remembrance

A place for family and friends to honour Hannah's memory with words, stories and prayers.

A tribute is a small way of saying: your life mattered, your love is remembered, and your place in our story remains. This page is dedicated to Hannah Nyambura Karanja, lovingly known as Wa Ruth.

A message of love

To those who loved Hannah: there is no perfect sentence for a loss this personal. You may write about a moment you shared, a lesson she taught, a prayer you remember, a quality you admired, or simply what you wish you could tell her today.

To her beloved grandchild, Hannah was more than a grandmother. She was like a mother, a person who understood and encouraged, and someone whose prayers helped nurture faith, hope and courage. That love is part of the reason this space exists.

“Those we love leave their fingerprints on our hearts.”

Tribute writing prompts

  • My favourite memory of Hannah is…
  • One thing she taught me was…
  • I will always remember the way she…
  • A prayer or blessing I want to share with the family is…
  • The part of her legacy I hope to carry forward is…

Share a tribute

Write with kindness and respect. Please avoid sharing private family information, unverified claims, or another person's story without permission. The website owner can review and edit submitted messages before publication.

Tributes & Remembrance

A place to prepare words of love, gratitude, prayer, and remembrance for Hannah.

A tribute can be a favourite memory, a lesson she taught, a prayer you remember, or simply a few words you wish you could tell her today. Hannah Nyambura Karanja, lovingly known as Wa Ruth, remains part of the stories and hearts of those who loved her.

How this works: You can write a tribute below, copy it, download it, or share it through WhatsApp. This GitHub Pages site does not have a shared database, so submissions are not automatically sent to the family or published for other visitors.

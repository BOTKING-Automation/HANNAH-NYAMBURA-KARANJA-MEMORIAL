Some people leave us with more than memories. They leave us with a way of seeing the world, a reason to keep hoping, and words that continue to guide us.

The gifts she left behind

Hannah Nyambura Karanja, lovingly known as Wa Ruth, is remembered as someone whose love and presence mattered deeply. To her beloved grandchild, she was more than a grandmother: she was like a mother, a trusted source of encouragement, and a person who understood them when life felt difficult.

She prayed, encouraged, and helped nurture a faith in God that became a source of hope and courage. Those gifts are part of the legacy her family can continue to carry.

Memories & Legacy

Love leaves traces in the stories we tell, the faith we carry, and the kindness we pass on.

A life is remembered not only through dates and milestones, but through the people who were loved, the prayers that were offered, the encouragement that was given, and the everyday moments that become precious.

The gifts she left behind

Hannah Nyambura Karanja, lovingly known as Wa Ruth, is remembered with deep love. To her beloved grandchild, she was more than a grandmother: she was like a mother, a trusted source of encouragement, and someone who understood them during difficult seasons.

She prayed and helped nurture a faith in God that became a source of hope and courage. Those gifts are part of the legacy her family can continue to carry—not by pretending that grief is easy, but by allowing love and faith to accompany them through it.

Making room for every memory

There is no single right way to remember someone. One person may recall a conversation; another may remember a prayer, a visit, a celebration, or the simple feeling of being welcomed. Some memories bring laughter. Others may bring tears. Both can be part of love.

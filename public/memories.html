Memories & Legacy

Love leaves traces everywhere: in the stories we repeat, the prayers we still remember, the lessons we return to, and the quiet ache when we reach for someone who is no longer beside us.

A life is remembered in the smallest things as much as the great milestones: in the sound of a familiar voice, in a piece of advice we still hear in our minds, in a prayer that carried us through, and in ordinary moments we would give so much to experience one more time.

The gifts she left behind

The Late Hannah Nyambura Karanja, lovingly known as Wa Ruth, is remembered with a love that grief cannot erase. To the family and friends who held her dear, she was more than a grandmother: she was a loving presence remembered for understanding, prayer, encouragement, and warmth. Every relative and friend will carry a different part of her story. Together, those memories show how deeply she was cherished and how much of her kindness continues in the lives she touched.

She prayed and helped nurture a faith in God that became a source of hope and courage. Now that her voice can no longer offer those words in person, the faith she helped plant can still be carried forward. No one has to pretend grief is easy. Love and sorrow can sit in the same heart, and faith can hold us even while tears fall.

Making room for every memory

There is no single right way to remember someone who mattered deeply. One person may miss a conversation, another may carry a prayer, a laugh, a visit, or the feeling of being welcomed without having to explain themselves. Some memories will make us smile through tears. Others may leave us quiet for a while. Both are love finding its way through grief.

THE MEMORIES THAT STAY

We would choose
those moments all over again.

Even knowing how painful goodbye would be, we would still choose the conversations, the lessons, the laughter, the prayers, and the love. A precious life is never made less meaningful by the pain of missing it.

When a memory comes, give it room. Say her name. Tell the story. Let the tears come if they must. Every tender memory is a small way of saying: you mattered, you were loved, and you are still part of us.

“Missing you is the price my heart pays for having been loved by you.”

For the Late Hannah Nyambura Karanja — our beloved Wa Ruth.

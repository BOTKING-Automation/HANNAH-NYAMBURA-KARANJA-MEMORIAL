A note for the family
This eulogy is lovingly drafted from the details shared so far. Before reading it publicly, please add any family-approved personal stories and confirm that every passage reflects Hannah and the family's wishes.

01 · OPENING WORDS

Family, friends, and everyone gathered in love: today, we speak the name Hannah Nyambura Karanja—our beloved Wa Ruth—with hearts that are grateful for her life and aching because we cannot keep her here. We gather because she mattered. We gather because love this deep deserves to be remembered.

There are losses that language cannot hold. We can say that someone has died, but those words cannot explain what it feels like to miss their voice, to wish for one more conversation, or to realise that the world will keep moving while our hearts are still trying to understand. Today, we mourn Hannah. We thank God that we were given the gift of her life, even as we grieve the unbearable fact that we must now remember her without being able to reach for her.

We come with tears that need no apology, memories that may make us smile and cry at once, and faith that we hold onto even when our hearts feel broken. Each person here carries a different part of Hannah, but all of us can honour the truth that her life mattered. The love she gave did not end with her last day; it remains in the people she comforted, the faith she helped plant, and the stories we will keep telling.

02 · MORE THAN A GRANDMOTHER

A heart that made
others feel understood.

To her family and the many people who held her dear, Hannah was far more than a name or a title. She was a beloved grandmother with a motherly heart, remembered for listening, encouraging, praying, and helping others feel less alone. Each person who knew her will carry a different memory, but all can understand the ache of wishing for one more conversation, one more smile, or one more ordinary day together. Her place in this family cannot be reduced to any single relationship; she belongs to the shared story of all who cherished her.

Her encouragement was not a small thing. A word spoken at the right moment, a prayer offered with love, or the simple feeling that someone understood could become a lifeline. Her encouragement helped people keep hope alive, and that kind of love can become an inner voice that comforts us long after a conversation has ended. The courage she offered can continue to travel through the family and community in the way people care for one another.

Every family member will carry their own memories: conversations, quiet moments, laughter, acts of care, and ordinary days that have become precious. Those memories are not small. They are pieces of a life shared, and they can be held close and passed on with tenderness.

03 · A WOMAN OF PRAYER AND FAITH

A faith she helped
plant and nurture.

One of the most precious gifts Hannah shared was faith in God. Through her prayers, words, and example, she helped point the people she loved toward a source of strength beyond themselves. She helped nurture a reason to pray when answers do not come easily and a source of hope when tomorrow feels uncertain. That gift is part of her legacy, carried in different ways by family members, friends, and all whose lives she touched.

That legacy matters especially in a season of grief. When the road ahead feels uncertain, the faith she helped nurture can be remembered and carried forward. When words are difficult to find, prayer can offer a place to bring sorrow honestly. And when someone feels weak, the love and encouragement she gave can inspire them to offer that same care to another person.

Faith does not mean we will never cry or that we must pretend the loss does not hurt. We can grieve honestly and still seek God. We can miss Hannah deeply and still give thanks for the years, memories, prayers, and lessons that remain with us.

04 · WHAT REMAINS

A legacy that love
will keep alive.

Death changes the way we experience someone we love, but it does not erase the meaning of their life. Hannah's influence continues in the heart she encouraged, in the prayers that are still remembered, in the faith she helped awaken, and in the love her family will carry into the future. There will be moments when her absence feels louder than anything else. In those moments, may the memory of how she loved remind us that something beautiful was here—and that the good she planted is still alive.

We cannot hold every moment still. What we can do is keep the memories, carry forward the lessons, tell the stories, and allow the best of what we received to shape the way we live. We can honour Hannah by encouraging one another, making room for each other's grief, choosing kindness, and continuing in faith.

Her legacy need not be measured only in the number of years she lived or in the milestones that can be listed. It can also be seen in the hearts she touched, the courage she helped build, and the love that remains present in the lives of those who remember her.

05 · A WORD TO THE FAMILY

May you find strength
in one another.

To the family, and to everyone who feels this loss: please be gentle with your own heart. There is no deadline for missing someone. There is no correct number of tears, no rule that says you must be strong every day, and no need to explain why a memory hurts. Some days you may laugh at a story and then cry because you wish you could tell it to her. Let both feelings belong. They are both signs of love.

May you find comfort in the stories you share. May Hannah's name continue to be spoken with tenderness around family gatherings and in quiet conversations. When one of you feels weak, may another help carry the burden. When the days feel long, may prayer, friendship, and the kindness of others remind you that you do not have to carry everything alone.

We are grateful for Hannah Nyambura Karanja—for her life, for the love she gave, for the prayers she offered, and for the lasting impression she leaves behind. We ask God to be near to everyone who mourns her and to give the family strength for each new day.

06 · FINAL FAREWELL

Thank you, Wa Ruth.
We will remember.

Wa Ruth, thank you for loving us in ways we will spend a lifetime remembering. Thank you for the prayers you prayed, the hope you gave, the courage you encouraged, and the faith you helped plant. Thank you for being the kind of grandmother whose love could feel like a mother's embrace. We wish we could have more time. We wish we could hear you again. Since we cannot, we will carry your love forward and do our best to let the good you planted keep growing.

As we say goodbye, we entrust you to God's eternal care and ask Him to hold those of us who must continue without your earthly presence. When we miss you, may He meet us in the silence. When memories hurt, may He remind us that the love behind them was real. When the days feel long, may the faith you helped nurture become a lamp for our next step. We cannot ask for one more day, so we thank God for the days we were given.

May you rest in the eternal peace of God, Wa Ruth. If love could have kept you here, you would never have left. Until we meet again, you will be loved beyond what words can hold, missed in ways that change from day to day, and remembered in every prayer, every lesson, and every heart you helped shape.

Amen.

A FAMILY TRIBUTE OF LOVE AND GRATITUDE

Our beloved Wa Ruth,
thank you for the life you shared with us.

We wish we could sit with you again, hear your voice, share our news, and thank you for the love you gave in countless ways. We wish for one more conversation, one more smile, and one more ordinary day together. These wishes come from the deep affection held by the many people whose lives you touched.

You shared faith, prayers, encouragement, and hope. Your example pointed people toward God and reminded us to care for one another through difficult seasons. The hardest part of goodbye is knowing that ordinary moments can become the ones we long for most.

We cannot bring back the days we shared, but we can honour them. We can keep praying, keep growing, show one another the patience and encouragement you taught through your example, and remember the faith you helped nurture. When life feels heavy, your memory can remind us to take one more step together.

“The love you gave us continues to hold us, even as we learn to live with your absence.”

With love, gratitude, and respect, your family and all who cherish your memory.

01 · OPENING WORDS

Family, friends, and everyone gathered in love: today, we come with heavy hearts to remember and give thanks for the life of Hannah Nyambura Karanja, lovingly known as Wa Ruth.

There are moments when words feel too small for what the heart is carrying. The loss of someone we love reminds us how precious a life can be, how deeply a person can touch us, and how difficult it can be to imagine the days ahead without their presence. Today, we mourn Hannah, and we also thank God for the gift of her life and for the love she shared.

We gather with sorrow, but not without hope. We bring our tears, our memories, our gratitude, and our faith. Each person here will remember Hannah in a different way, yet we are joined by the truth that her life mattered and that the love she gave will continue to mean something long after this day.

02 · MORE THAN A GRANDMOTHER

A heart that made
others feel understood.

To the grandchild who loved her deeply, Hannah was more than a grandmother. She was like a mother: someone who understood, encouraged, and offered hope. There is something unforgettable about a person who makes you feel seen, who listens to your heart, and who reminds you that you are not walking through life alone. That is the special place Hannah holds in the heart of the person who loved her in this way.

Her encouragement was a gift. When life felt difficult, being understood by someone who cared could make the burden feel less lonely. A loving word, a prayer, a reminder to keep going—these things may seem small to the world, but to the person receiving them, they can become a source of strength.

Every family member will carry their own memories: conversations, quiet moments, laughter, acts of care, and ordinary days that have become precious. Those memories are not small. They are pieces of a life shared, and they can be held close and passed on with tenderness.

03 · A WOMAN OF PRAYER AND FAITH

A faith she helped
plant and nurture.

One of the greatest gifts Hannah gave was the gift of faith. She introduced a beloved grandchild to God and helped nurture a foundation of faith, hope, and courage. Her prayers and encouragement pointed beyond the struggles of the present moment toward the possibility of finding strength in God.

That legacy matters especially in a season of grief. When the road ahead feels uncertain, the faith she helped nurture can be remembered and carried forward. When words are difficult to find, prayer can offer a place to bring sorrow honestly. And when someone feels weak, the love and encouragement she gave can inspire them to offer that same care to another person.

Faith does not mean we will never cry or that we must pretend the loss does not hurt. We can grieve honestly and still seek God. We can miss Hannah deeply and still give thanks for the years, memories, prayers, and lessons that remain with us.

04 · WHAT REMAINS

A legacy that love
will keep alive.

Death changes the way we experience someone we love, but it does not erase the meaning of their life. Hannah's influence continues in the heart she encouraged, in the prayers she prayed, in the faith she helped awaken, and in the love that her family can continue to share.

We cannot hold every moment still. What we can do is keep the memories, carry forward the lessons, tell the stories, and allow the best of what we received to shape the way we live. We can honour Hannah by encouraging one another, making room for each other's grief, choosing kindness, and continuing in faith.

Her legacy need not be measured only in the number of years she lived or in the milestones that can be listed. It can also be seen in the hearts she touched, the courage she helped build, and the love that remains present in the lives of those who remember her.

05 · A WORD TO THE FAMILY

May you find strength
in one another.

To the family and everyone who feels this loss: may you be gentle with yourselves and with one another. Grief does not follow one timetable, and there is no single correct way to mourn. Some days may bring tears; other days may bring a smile when a memory returns. Both can belong to love.

May you find comfort in the stories you share. May Hannah's name continue to be spoken with tenderness around family gatherings and in quiet conversations. When one of you feels weak, may another help carry the burden. When the days feel long, may prayer, friendship, and the kindness of others remind you that you do not have to carry everything alone.

We are grateful for Hannah Nyambura Karanja—for her life, for the love she gave, for the prayers she offered, and for the lasting impression she leaves behind. We ask God to be near to everyone who mourns her and to give the family strength for each new day.

06 · FINAL FAREWELL

Thank you, Wa Ruth.
We will remember.

Hannah, thank you for the love you gave, the faith you shared, and the courage you helped build. Thank you for the prayers and encouragement that became part of the lives of those who love you. Your place in our hearts remains, and the good you planted can continue to grow through us.

As we say goodbye, we entrust you into God's hands and ask Him to comfort those who remain. May the memories of your life bring gratitude even through our tears. May your loved ones find peace one day at a time, and may the faith you helped nurture continue to be a light along the way.

May you rest in God's eternal peace. Until we meet again, you will forever be loved, forever remembered, and forever part of us.

Amen.

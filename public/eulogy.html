Family, friends, and everyone gathered here today: we come together with heavy hearts to say farewell to a very special woman, Hannah Nyambura Karanja, lovingly known as Wa Ruth.

There are moments when words feel too small for what the heart is carrying. Losing someone we love reminds us how precious a life can be, how deeply a person can touch us, and how difficult it is to imagine the days ahead without their presence. Today, we mourn Hannah, but we also give thanks to God for the gift of her life and for the love she shared with us.

More than a grandmother

To those who loved her closely, Hannah was more than a grandmother. She was like a mother — someone who could understand, encourage, and offer hope. There is something unforgettable about a person who makes you feel seen, who listens to your heart, and who reminds you that you are not walking through life alone. That is part of the place Hannah holds in our hearts.

Each of us will carry our own memories of her. Some memories may be made of conversations, some of quiet moments, some of laughter, and some of the ordinary things that become precious only when we look back. Whatever form those memories take, they are now treasures we can carry with us.

A woman of prayer and faith

One of the greatest gifts Hannah gave was the gift of faith. Through her prayers and the way she introduced loved ones to God, she helped plant seeds of belief, hope, and courage. Her faith was not simply something to speak about; it was something she shared with the people she loved. Her prayers reminded us to look beyond our immediate troubles and to seek strength in God.

Today, that legacy matters. When grief feels overwhelming, we can remember the faith she encouraged. When the road ahead feels uncertain, we can hold on to the hope she helped nurture. And when we miss her, we can honour her by continuing to pray, to love, to encourage one another, and to live out the good she planted in us.

“The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.”PSALM 34:18

What remains

Death changes the way we experience someone we love, but it does not erase the meaning of their life. Hannah's influence continues in the hearts she encouraged, in the prayers she prayed, in the faith she helped awaken, and in the love her family will continue to share. We cannot keep every moment from passing, but we can keep the lessons, the memories, and the love alive.

As we say goodbye, we do not need to pretend that this is easy. We can grieve honestly. We can miss her deeply. We can lean on one another and ask God to give us strength for each new day. There is no single way to mourn, and every tear is a reflection of love.

A word to the family

To the family and everyone who feels this loss: may you be gentle with yourselves and with one another. May you find comfort in the memories you share. May the stories of Hannah continue to be told around family gatherings, in quiet conversations, and in the lives of younger generations. When one of you feels weak, may another help carry the burden. And may the faith Hannah helped nurture be a source of comfort in the days ahead.

We thank God for Hannah Nyambura Karanja — for her life, for the love she gave, for her prayers, and for the lasting impression she leaves behind. We entrust her into God's hands and ask Him to comfort all who mourn her.

Hannah, thank you for the love you gave, the faith you shared, and the courage you helped build. You will be remembered with love and gratitude. Your place in our hearts remains, and the good you planted will continue to grow.

May you rest in God's eternal peace. Until we meet again, you will forever be loved, forever remembered, and forever part of us.

Amen.

A beloved grandmother — and more

To one grandchild in particular, Hannah was more than a grandmother. She was like a mother: a person who understood, encouraged, and offered comfort when life was heavy. To be understood by someone who truly cares is a precious gift. Her place in that heart cannot be measured by a title alone; it is measured by the comfort she gave, the courage she encouraged, and the love that remains.

Every family member and friend will have their own way of remembering her. Some memories may be vivid and full of laughter; others may be quiet moments that only become precious with time. Each story, photograph, lesson, and act of kindness can become part of the family’s shared remembrance.

A life that pointed toward God

Faith was one of the most meaningful gifts Hannah passed on. She introduced her beloved grandchild to God, prayed, and helped nurture faith, hope, and courage. Her example made faith more than words: it became a place to turn when life was uncertain, a reason to keep praying, and a reminder that hardship does not have to have the final word.

Grief does not erase faith. Nor does faith mean that a person must hide their tears. We can mourn honestly, ask difficult questions, remember deeply, and still turn toward God for strength one day at a time.

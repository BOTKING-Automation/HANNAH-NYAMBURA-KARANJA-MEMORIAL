The Story of
Hannah Nyambura Karanja

Lovingly known as Wa Ruth — the grandmother whose love felt like a mother's embrace and whose faith still speaks to the heart.

7 October 2026 · In loving memory

Some people are woven so deeply into our lives that we no longer know where their love ends and our own courage begins. Their words become the comfort we carry, their prayers become shelter in difficult seasons, and their faith points us toward God. Hannah Nyambura Karanja, lovingly known as Wa Ruth, was that kind of person. Her love did not simply make life brighter; it helped the people closest to her keep going.

A beloved grandmother — and more

To one grandchild in particular, Hannah was more than a grandmother. She was like a mother—a heart that listened, a voice that encouraged, and a person who could make a difficult world feel less lonely. Being truly understood is a rare gift. Her place in that heart cannot be measured by a family title; it is measured by all the times her love brought comfort, her words restored courage, and her presence reminded someone that they were not forgotten.

Every family member and friend will have their own way of remembering her. Some memories may be vivid and full of laughter; others may be quiet moments that only become precious with time. Each story, photograph, lesson, and act of kindness can become part of the family’s shared remembrance.

A life that pointed toward God

One of the deepest gifts Hannah gave was helping a beloved grandchild know God. Through her prayers and encouragement, she planted seeds of faith, hope, and courage that can keep growing even now that she is gone. That gift is more than a memory; it is a light to carry into days she will not be here to witness. When life hurts, the faith she helped nurture can still whisper: pray again, take the next step, and do not give up.

Grief does not erase faith. Nor does faith mean that a person must hide their tears. We can mourn honestly, ask difficult questions, remember deeply, and still turn toward God for strength one day at a time.

A WORD FROM THE HEART

Dear Wa Ruth,
your love still guides me.

There are days when a memory of you arrives so gently, and days when missing you feels like a weight no words can lift. I wish I could sit with you again, hear your voice, tell you what is on my heart, and thank you for understanding me in ways I may never fully be able to explain.

You helped me know God. You gave me faith to hold, hope to reach for, and courage to keep moving. I cannot return to those moments, but I can carry what you planted. Your love is still part of the person I am becoming.

“Some people leave this world, but never leave the hearts they helped build.”

Until we meet again, Wa Ruth. I will carry your love with me.

Family &
Acknowledgements

Honouring the people who carry her memory and thanking those who stand with the family.

The loss of someone loved touches many hearts. This page offers space to acknowledge Hannah Nyambura Karanja, lovingly known as Wa Ruth, and to express gratitude to relatives, friends, neighbours, church members and everyone who offers care during this season of mourning.

To the family

May the family find room for every kind of grief and every treasured memory. Each person may carry a different part of Hannah's story, and each person's relationship with her is worthy of respect. May relatives listen to one another with patience, speak her name with tenderness, and support one another through the days ahead.

For one beloved grandchild, Hannah was like a mother: someone who understood, encouraged, prayed and helped nurture faith, hope and courage. That bond is part of the family story and a reminder that love can leave a lasting influence across generations.

With sincere gratitude

We offer heartfelt thanks to everyone who has reached out with a message, prayer, visit, practical help, kind word or act of compassion. Thank you to those who help with arrangements, share memories, support the family quietly, and give others space to mourn in their own way.

If you have supported the family and your name should be acknowledged publicly, please add it here only after confirming the correct spelling and receiving permission where needed.

Continuing her legacy together

One way to honour Hannah is to keep offering the care she gave: listen to someone who needs understanding, pray for a person going through hardship, encourage a loved one who feels discouraged, and make room for family stories to be passed to the next generation.

May the family be strengthened by the love around them. May God grant wisdom for practical decisions, comfort in sorrow, and peace for each new day.

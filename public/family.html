Family &
Acknowledgements

Honouring the family who must learn to live with her absence, and thanking everyone whose love helps them through it.

When someone as precious as the Late Hannah Nyambura Karanja leaves this world, the loss reaches far beyond a single person. It is felt in the rooms where she is missed, in the family stories that now carry a new tenderness, and in the hearts that still wish they could reach for her. This page honours Wa Ruth and gives thanks to everyone who surrounds her family with prayer, patience and compassion.

To the family

May the family be gentle with one another. Some will cry openly; some will grow quiet; some may laugh at a memory and then feel the tears come. Let there be room for every kind of grief. No one should have to prove how deeply they loved her by hiding their sorrow. May Hannah's name remain welcome in conversation and her memory bring the family closer, not farther apart.

Within her family, Hannah held a place of deep love and respect. As a grandmother with a motherly heart, she offered care, prayer, encouragement, and the feeling of being understood. Every relative carries a unique bond with her, and all those bonds belong in this memorial. The faith, hope, and courage she helped nurture can continue to guide her family and the wider community for generations.

With sincere gratitude

We offer heartfelt thanks to everyone who has reached out with a message, prayer, visit, practical help, kind word or act of compassion. Thank you to those who help with arrangements, share memories, support the family quietly, and give others space to mourn in their own way. In grief, small acts of care become memories too: a call at the right time, a prayer whispered on someone's behalf, a hand that stays when there is nothing to say.

If you have supported the family and your name should be acknowledged publicly, please add it here only after confirming the correct spelling and receiving permission where needed.

Continuing her legacy together

One way to honour Hannah is to let her love keep moving through the family: listen the way she listened, encourage the way she encouraged, pray for the person who is struggling, and tell the younger generations the stories that make them feel connected to her. The most beautiful memorial is not only the one we build online; it is the kindness we continue to give because she was here.

May the family be strengthened by the love around them. May God grant wisdom for practical decisions, comfort in sorrow, and peace for each new day.

FOR THE FAMILY SHE LOVED

May nobody have to
carry the missing alone.

There will be days when the family finds strength in one another, and days when one person feels the loss more sharply than they can explain. May love make room for both. May every member of the family be allowed to grieve in their own time and still feel held by the people around them.

May Wa Ruth's memory remain a blessing in this family. May the faith she helped nurture guide those who feel lost, and may the love she gave continue to travel from one generation to the next.

“We honour her best when we love one another with the care she helped teach us.”

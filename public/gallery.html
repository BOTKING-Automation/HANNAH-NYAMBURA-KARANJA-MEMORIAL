FOR THE MOMENTS WE WISH WE COULD REVISIT

We cannot return to the moment,
but we can hold its memory.

There may be photographs that bring a smile before the tears arrive. There may be others you cannot look at for long just yet. There is room for both. Take your time, and let each image bring back the love without demanding that your heart be ready for every feeling at once.

May these pictures become more than records of the past. May they remind us that Hannah was here, that she was loved, and that her life left marks of tenderness that time cannot erase.

“A picture holds one moment still; love keeps that moment alive.”

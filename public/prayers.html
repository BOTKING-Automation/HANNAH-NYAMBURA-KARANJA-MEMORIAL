Faith, Scripture
& Prayer

A quiet place to bring your tears to God, remember the faith Hannah helped nurture, and find words for the days when you miss her more than you can explain.

The Late Hannah Nyambura Karanja, lovingly known as Wa Ruth, shared faith, prayer, hope, and courage with the people she loved. This page honours that sacred gift. It is for the nights when memories are loud, the mornings when her absence is the first thing the heart notices, and the moments when all we can manage is a whispered prayer.

When the heart is broken

“The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.”PSALM 34:18

Grief can arrive without warning. A photograph can stop us in our tracks; an ordinary quiet evening can suddenly feel unbearably empty; a happy memory can hurt because we cannot return to it. We do not need to tidy our sorrow before coming to God. We can bring Him the tears, the questions, the anger, the gratitude and the ache of wishing we had one more moment.

Hope beyond today

“Blessed are those who mourn, for they will be comforted.”MATTHEW 5:4

Jesus' words do not dismiss sorrow; they acknowledge it and point toward God's comfort. Mourning takes time, and people grieve differently. Give yourself and others patience. A difficult day does not mean faith has failed.

Strength for each new day

“I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.”PHILIPPIANS 4:13

This verse is a reminder to seek strength in Christ, especially when the next step feels small. Strength may look like getting through an hour, accepting support, praying honestly, resting, or speaking to someone who listens.

A prayer for the family

Heavenly Father,

We come before You with hearts that are grieving the loss of the Late Hannah Nyambura Karanja, lovingly known as Wa Ruth. Thank You for the gift of her life, for the love she shared, for the prayers she offered, and for the faith and courage she helped nurture.

Please draw near to every member of the family and every friend who feels this loss. Comfort those who are overwhelmed by sorrow. Give strength to those who must keep going while their hearts are hurting. Help the family be patient with one another, to share memories with tenderness, and to receive support without shame.

When the days feel lonely, remind us that we may bring our grief honestly before You. Let the good Hannah planted continue to bear fruit through acts of kindness, prayer, encouragement and love. Guide the family through each day, and hold them in Your peace.

In Jesus' name, Amen.

A short prayer for difficult moments

Lord Jesus, we miss Hannah. Some moments we are grateful for every memory; other moments we wish we could hear her voice just one more time. Meet us in the ache we cannot fix. Hold what feels too heavy for this family. Quiet our thoughts, steady our hearts, and give us enough strength for the next small step. Thank You for letting her love touch our lives. Help us carry that love forward without pretending we do not miss her. Amen.

Remembering through faith

Hannah's faith legacy can continue in simple ways: reading a Psalm, praying for a family member, encouraging someone who feels alone, or remembering the person who first helped us look toward God. These acts do not make grief disappear. They give the love she planted somewhere to grow.

If sorrow feels unbearable or begins to make everyday life impossible, reach out to a trusted person, pastor, counsellor or health professional. Seeking support is not a failure of faith.

A PRAYER FOR THE EMPTY PLACES

Lord, hold the hearts
that miss her.

Father, You know the places in our hearts that feel different now. You know the memories that make us smile and the moments when the tears return. Please sit with this family in every quiet room, every difficult morning, every first holiday, and every ordinary day when they suddenly wish Hannah were here.

Thank You for the woman who helped plant faith, hope, and courage. Let her love continue through the kindness we show, the prayers we offer, and the way we care for one another. When grief is too deep for words, hear even the silence. When strength feels far away, carry us for a while.

“The Lord is near to all who call on Him, to all who call on Him in truth.”

In Jesus' name, Amen.

A prayer for the family

Heavenly Father,

We come before You with hearts that are grieving and memories that are precious. Thank You for the life of Hannah Nyambura Karanja, for the love she gave, for the prayers she offered, and for the faith and courage she helped nurture in those she loved.

Be close to every member of the family and every friend who feels this loss. Give comfort when the days feel heavy, strength for the moments ahead, and peace that does not depend on having every answer. Help us honour her memory through kindness, faith, patience, and love for one another.

May the hope found in You sustain us, and may the love she shared continue to bear fruit in the lives of those who remember her.

In Jesus’ name, Amen.

Faith, Scripture
& Prayer

A quiet place to remember the faith Hannah helped nurture and seek comfort in God's presence.

Hannah Nyambura Karanja, lovingly known as Wa Ruth, helped nurture faith, hope and courage in the life of a beloved grandchild. This page honours that gift and offers words of Scripture and prayer for days when grief feels difficult to carry.

When the heart is broken

“The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.”PSALM 34:18

Grief can come in waves. A familiar song, a photograph, a quiet evening or a family gathering may suddenly remind us of the person we miss. We do not need to hide our sadness from God. We can bring Him our tears, our questions, our gratitude and our need for strength.

Hope beyond today

“Blessed are those who mourn, for they will be comforted.”MATTHEW 5:4

Jesus' words do not dismiss sorrow; they acknowledge it and point toward God's comfort. Mourning takes time, and people grieve differently. Give yourself and others patience. A difficult day does not mean faith has failed.

Strength for each new day

“I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.”PHILIPPIANS 4:13

This verse is a reminder to seek strength in Christ, especially when the next step feels small. Strength may look like getting through an hour, accepting support, praying honestly, resting, or speaking to someone who listens.

A prayer for the family

Heavenly Father,

We come before You with hearts that are grieving the loss of Hannah Nyambura Karanja, lovingly known as Wa Ruth. Thank You for the gift of her life, for the love she shared, for the prayers she offered, and for the faith and courage she helped nurture.

Please draw near to every member of the family and every friend who feels this loss. Comfort those who are overwhelmed by sorrow. Give strength to those who must keep going while their hearts are hurting. Help the family be patient with one another, to share memories with tenderness, and to receive support without shame.

When the days feel lonely, remind us that we may bring our grief honestly before You. Let the good Hannah planted continue to bear fruit through acts of kindness, prayer, encouragement and love. Guide the family through each day, and hold them in Your peace.

In Jesus' name, Amen.

A short prayer for difficult moments

Lord Jesus, I miss someone I love. Please meet me in this moment. Quiet my anxious thoughts, help me breathe, and give me enough strength for the next step. Thank You for the love I was able to receive. Help me carry it forward. Amen.

Remembering through faith

Hannah's faith legacy can continue in simple ways: reading a Psalm, praying for a family member, encouraging someone who feels alone, attending church when ready, or remembering a lesson she shared. These acts do not remove grief, but they can help love continue to take shape.

If sorrow feels unbearable or begins to make everyday life impossible, reach out to a trusted person, pastor, counsellor or health professional. Seeking support is not a failure of faith.
